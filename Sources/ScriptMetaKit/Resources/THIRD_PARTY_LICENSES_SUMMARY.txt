SCRIPTMETAKit
=============

Developed by Yoshiteru Murakami, the author of Scripta! and ACEMenuPlus,
SCRIPTMETAKit is a cross-platform library for macOS and Windows. It is
specialized in reading SCRIPTMETA tags and discovering and listing scripts.
